Place royalty-free background music (.mp3/.m4a/.ogg/.wav) in this folder.
The cinematic director picks one track at random and mixes it under the voiceover at ~10% volume.

Files in this folder are git-ignored (see .gitignore).

Free sources:
- YouTube Audio Library (studio.youtube.com -> Audio Library)
- Pixabay Music
- Free Music Archive (check each licence)
